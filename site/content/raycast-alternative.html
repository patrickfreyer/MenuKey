JumpKey is a free, open-source alternative to Raycast for the things most people use a launcher for every day: running menu commands, switching windows, and opening files and folders. It's a much smaller app. If you rely on Raycast's extensions, AI chat, snippets or clipboard history, keep Raycast.

What JumpKey replaces

  • Search Menu Bar Items: press ⌥ Space in any app and type to run any menu command. Each result shows its keyboard shortcut.
  • File search: files and folders ranked by how often and how recently you open them, with zoxide's history included. ↩ opens, ⌘ ↩ reveals in Finder or opens a folder in your terminal.
  • Switch Windows: search the open windows of every app on your current desktop and jump to one, with an optional small preview.
  • Menu bar icons: open Wi‑Fi, Battery, Control Center or any app's icon by name.
  • Plain English: when nothing matches, Apple's on-device model turns "make it bigger" into View › Zoom In. It runs only as a fallback and never leaves your Mac.

What it doesn't replace

  • Extensions and the Raycast Store
  • Snippets and text expansion
  • Clipboard history (the macOS 26 Spotlight has one built in)
  • Window layouts and resizing
  • AI chat, notes, calculator and the other built-in tools

Why choose an open-source launcher

JumpKey's source is on GitHub under the MIT licence, so you can read exactly what it does with the Accessibility permission it needs. It has no account, no analytics and makes no network requests. The whole app is about 2,000 lines of Swift, and the download is under 1 MB.

Other Raycast alternatives

  • Spotlight in macOS 26: built in, with app, file, action and clipboard views. Enough for many people.
  • Alfred: a long-standing launcher with a free version and a one-time Powerpack licence. See Raycast vs Alfred vs Spotlight.

Questions

Is there a free alternative to Raycast?

Raycast itself is free for its core features. If you want a smaller open-source tool for menu search, window switching and file search, JumpKey is free under the MIT licence. Spotlight in macOS 26 is the built-in option.

Can I use JumpKey and Raycast together?

Yes. Give them different shortcuts. JumpKey uses Option-Space by default, and you can change it from its menu bar icon.

Does JumpKey need an account or internet connection?

No. It has no account and makes no network requests. Plain-English search uses Apple's on-device model.
